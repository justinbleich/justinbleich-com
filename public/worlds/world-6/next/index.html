World 6 · Chapter one

Ashgrove Reading Room

You arrive after midnight. The fire is still lit, and the study looks as if someone left in a hurry.

Drag to look around. Tap anything that glints.

The cabinet on the east wall keeps its own counsel. It answers only to the hours, taken in their proper order.

Find the canvas above the hearth and read the numeral beneath each emblem. Then set the dials left to right: first hour, second hour, third hour, fourth.
